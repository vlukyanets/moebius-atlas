---
title: Morley's Trisector Theorem
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 1899
difficulty: 5
requires: [law-of-sines, triangle-cevians]
---

Trisect every angle of an arbitrary triangle. The three intersection points of adjacent trisectors are the vertices of an equilateral triangle with side

$$
8R\sin\frac{\alpha}{3}\sin\frac{\beta}{3}\sin\frac{\gamma}{3}
$$

The result is striking because trisection itself is not constructible with compass and straightedge.
