Each coordinate of the midpoint is the half-sum of the corresponding coordinates of the ends:

$$
x_0 = \frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\quad y_0 = \frac{y_1+y_2}{2},\quad z_0 = \frac{z_1+z_2}{2}
$$
