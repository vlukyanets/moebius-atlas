---
title: Areas and Volumes of Similar Solids
tag: THM
track: school
grade: 11
requires: [solid-similarity, prism-volume, similar-areas]
---

Under a similarity with ratio $k$ areas grow $k^2$ times and volumes $k^3$ times:

$$
\frac{S_1}{S_2} = k^2, \qquad \frac{V_1}{V_2} = k^3
$$

Doubling a model multiplies its weight by eight.
