The point of the unit circle obtained by a rotation through $\alpha$ has coordinates $(\cos\alpha; \sin\alpha)$, and

$$
\tan\alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}, \qquad \cot\alpha = \frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}
$$

The acute-angle definitions become the special case $0 < \alpha < 90^\circ$.
