title: Area of a Curvilinear Trapezoid
tag: MTD

grade: 11

---

For $f \ge 0$ the figure bounded by the graph, the axis and the two vertical lines has area $S = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx$; the area between two graphs is the integral of their difference.
