Powers with the same base add their exponents when multiplied and subtract them when divided; a power of a power multiplies them:

$$
a^m a^n = a^{m+n}, \qquad \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}, \qquad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}
$$

These three rules are what force the definitions $a^0 = 1$ and $a^{-n} = 1/a^n$.
