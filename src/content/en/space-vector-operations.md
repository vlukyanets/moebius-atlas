Coordinates add and scale componentwise:

$$
\vec{a} \pm \vec{b} = (a_1 \pm b_1;\, a_2 \pm b_2;\, a_3 \pm b_3), \qquad k\vec{a} = (ka_1;\, ka_2;\, ka_3)
$$

Vectors are collinear when their coordinates are proportional.
