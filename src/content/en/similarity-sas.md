---
title: "Similarity Criterion: Two Sides & Angle"
tag: THM
year: -300
difficulty: 2
requires: [similarity-aa]
---

If two sides of one triangle are proportional to two sides of another and the angles between these sides are equal, the triangles are similar:

$$
\frac{A_1B_1}{AB} = \frac{A_1C_1}{AC},\; \angle A_1 = \angle A \;\Rightarrow\; \triangle A_1B_1C_1 \sim \triangle ABC
$$
