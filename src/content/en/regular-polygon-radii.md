For a regular polygon with $n$ sides of length $a$, the radii of the circumscribed and inscribed circles are:

$$
R = \frac{a}{2\sin(180^\circ/n)},\quad r = \frac{a}{2\,\mathrm{tg}(180^\circ/n)}
$$
