Each conic is also a locus tied to its foci: for an ellipse the sum of the focal distances is constant, for a hyperbola the difference is, and a parabola keeps the distance to the focus equal to the distance to the directrix:

$$
r_1 + r_2 = 2a, \qquad |r_1 - r_2| = 2a, \qquad r = d
$$

The reflection property follows: a ray from one focus of an ellipse arrives at the other.
