## Why It Matters

Juice comes in $0.4$-litre glasses - how many from a $6$-litre jug? A
board $3.6$ m long is cut into $0.45$-m pieces - how many? Petrol is
$52.5$ hryvnias a litre and you paid $840$ - how many litres? Each of
these divides *by* a decimal, and nobody knows how to divide by four
tenths of something directly. The trick is not to: turn the divisor into
a natural number first, and then do what you already know.

## The Big Idea

A quotient doesn't change if you multiply the dividend and the divisor
by the same number. $6 : 2 = 3$, and $60 : 20 = 3$, and $600 : 200 = 3$ -
scaling both by ten leaves the answer alone, because you're asking the
same question in smaller units. So $6 : 0.4$ is the same as $60 : 4$:
multiply both by $10$, and the divisor becomes the natural number $4$.
Now it's a division from the last topic, and the answer is $15$.

Multiplying by $10$, $100$, $1000$ is just moving the point - so the
whole method is "move both points the same number of places, until the
divisor has none."

## The Rule

To divide by a decimal:

1. **Count the decimal places in the divisor.**
2. **Move the point in both the dividend and the divisor that many
   places to the right** - the divisor becomes a natural number. Add
   zeros to the dividend if it runs out of digits.
3. **Divide by the natural number** as in the last topic.

$$
6 : 0.4 = 60 : 4 = 15, \qquad 3.6 : 0.45 = 360 : 45 = 8
$$

Check: quotient times divisor is the dividend - $15 \cdot 0.4 = 6$.

Dividing by $0.1$, $0.01$, $0.001$ moves the point *right* by $1$, $2$, $3$
places - it's the same as multiplying by $10$, $100$, $1000$: $7.2 : 0.01
= 720$. And dividing by a number less than $1$ makes the result *bigger*
than the dividend: six litres hold more than six glasses.

## Worked Example

Find $840 : 52.5$.

- **Decimal places in the divisor:** $52.5$ has one.
- **Move both points one place right:** $52.5 \to 525$, and $840 \to
  8400$ - a zero was added, since $840$ had no digits after its point.
- **Divide:** $8400 : 525$. $525 \cdot 16 = 8400$ exactly.

So $840 : 52.5 = 16$ litres. Check: $16 \cdot 52.5 = 840$.

Now $0.0315 : 0.07$. Divisor has two places, so move both points two
right: $3.15 : 7$. Whole part $3 : 7$ doesn't go - $0$, point; bring down
$1$: $31 : 7 = 4$ rem. $3$; bring down $5$: $35 : 7 = 5$. Answer: $0.45$.

## Common Mistake

Moving the point in only one of the numbers. Turn $6 : 0.4$ into $6 : 4$
and you get $1.5$ - ten times too small, and obviously wrong, since six
litres surely fill more than one and a half glasses. The quotient only
stays the same if *both* numbers are scaled by the same amount. Whatever
you do to the divisor, do to the dividend.

## The Takeaway

Never divide by a decimal directly: slide the points of both numbers
right, the same number of places, until the divisor is a natural number,
then divide as usual. Dividing by a number below $1$ makes the result
larger, so the answer should be bigger than what you started with.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $9 : 0.3$.
2. Find $4.8 : 1.2$.
3. Find $0.56 : 0.08$.
4. Find $3.5 : 0.001$.
5. A $3.6$-m board is cut into $0.45$-m pieces. How many pieces?
6. Someone finds $6 : 0.4 = 1.5$. What did they do wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $90 : 3 = 30$.
> 2. $48 : 12 = 4$.
> 3. $56 : 8 = 7$.
> 4. $3500$.
> 5. $360 : 45 = 8$ pieces.
> 6. Only the divisor's point was moved; scaling both gives $60 : 4 =
>    15$.
