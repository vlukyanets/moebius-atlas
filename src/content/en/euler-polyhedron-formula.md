---
title: Euler's Formula for Polyhedra
tag: THM
track: school

year: 1758
requires: [polyhedron]
---

For every convex polyhedron

$$
V - E + F = 2,
$$

where $V$, $E$ and $F$ are the numbers of vertices, edges and faces. The count does not depend on the shape at all - only on the fact that the surface closes up without holes.
