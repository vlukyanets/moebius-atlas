## Why It Matters

A taxi charges $40$ hryvnias plus $12$ per kilometre: $C = 12k + 40$. A
candle $20$ cm tall burns $2$ cm an hour: $h = 20 - 2t$. Steady change -
the same amount added or taken every step - is the simplest kind of
change there is, and its graph is always a straight line. Linear
functions describe fares, wages, fuel, cooling, and every process
that moves at a constant rate.

## The Big Idea

$y = kx + b$: start at $b$, and for every unit $x$ grows, add $k$. The
graph is a straight line because the steps are always the same size -
up $k$ for every one across. The number $k$ is the slope: positive and
the line climbs to the right, negative and it falls, zero and it's
flat. The number $b$ is where the line crosses the $y$-axis, since at
$x = 0$, $y = b$.

Two points fix a line, so two points fix the graph - compute $y$ at two
convenient $x$'s, plot, and draw the line through them. The rest is
reading: where it crosses the $x$-axis is the zero of the function,
which is the root of the linear equation $kx + b = 0$.

## The Rule

A **linear function** is a function of the form

$$
y = kx + b
$$

where $k$ and $b$ are numbers. Its domain is all numbers.

Its graph is a **straight line**. To draw it, find two points and join
them.

- $k$ is the **slope** (angular coefficient): for $k > 0$ the function
  is increasing (the line rises to the right); for $k < 0$ it is
  decreasing; for $k = 0$ it's constant, $y = b$, a horizontal line.
- $b$ is the $y$-intercept: the graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0; b)$.
- The zero of the function is $x = -\frac{b}{k}$ (for $k \neq 0$): where
  the line crosses the $x$-axis.

The special case $b = 0$, $y = kx$, is **direct proportionality**: a
line through the origin.

Two lines $y = k_1x + b_1$ and $y = k_2x + b_2$ are parallel when $k_1 =
k_2$ and $b_1 \neq b_2$; they intersect when $k_1 \neq k_2$.

## Worked Example

Plot $y = -2x + 3$; find its zero, say whether it increases or
decreases, and find where it meets $y = x - 3$.

- **Two points:** $x = 0$ gives $y = 3$; $x = 2$ gives $y = -1$. Plot
  $(0; 3)$ and $(2; -1)$, draw the line.
- **Zero:** $-2x + 3 = 0$, $x = 1.5$. The line crosses the $x$-axis at
  $(1.5; 0)$ - check on the drawing.
- **Direction:** $k = -2 < 0$, decreasing - the line falls to the right.
- **Meeting $y = x - 3$:** at the intersection the $y$'s are equal:
  $-2x + 3 = x - 3$, so $3x = 6$, $x = 2$, $y = -1$. They meet at $(2; -1)$.

The candle: $h = 20 - 2t$. Slope $-2$, decreasing; $h = 0$ at $t = 10$ - it
burns out after $10$ hours.

## Common Mistake

Swapping $k$ and $b$. In $y = 3 - 2x$ the slope is $-2$ and the intercept
is $3$ - the order on the page doesn't decide; the number multiplying
$x$ is the slope. And a line through $(0; b)$ with $b \neq 0$ is not
direct proportionality: doubling $x$ doesn't double $y$ when there's a
$b$ - the taxi fare for $2$ km is not twice the fare for $1$ km.

## The Takeaway

$y = kx + b$ draws as a straight line: $k$ sets the steepness and
direction, $b$ sets where it crosses the $y$-axis. Two points draw it;
$kx + b = 0$ finds where it crosses the $x$-axis; equal slopes mean
parallel lines.

## Check Yourself

1. Name $k$ and $b$ in $y = 5 - 0.5x$. Increasing or decreasing?
2. Plot $y = 2x - 4$ and find its zero.
3. Which of these graphs pass through the origin: $y = 3x$, $y = 3x + 1$,
   $y = -x$?
4. Are the lines $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = 2x - 5$ parallel? And $y = 2x + 1$
   and $y = -2x + 1$?
5. A taxi charges $40 + 12k$. Draw the graph for $k$ from $0$ to $5$ and
   read the fare for $3$ km.
6. Someone says the slope of $y = 4 + 7x$ is $4$. Correct them.

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $k = -0.5$, $b = 5$; decreasing.
> 2. Through $(0; -4)$ and $(2; 0)$; zero at $x = 2$.
> 3. $y = 3x$ and $y = -x$.
> 4. Yes; no - they intersect at $(0; 1)$.
> 5. A line from $(0; 40)$ rising $12$ per km; at $3$ km, $76$ hryvnias.
> 6. The slope is the coefficient of $x$: $7$; $4$ is the intercept.
