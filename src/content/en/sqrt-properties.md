The root of a product is the product of the roots, and the same holds for a quotient:

$$
\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a}\,\sqrt{b}, \qquad \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} = \frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}
$$

For a sum there is no such rule.
