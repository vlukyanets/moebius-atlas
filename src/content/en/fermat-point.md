---
title: Fermat Point
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 1640
difficulty: 4
requires: [rotation-motion, ptolemy-theorem]
---

In a triangle with all angles below $120^\circ$ the point minimising the sum $PA + PB + PC$ sees all three sides at equal angles of $120^\circ$.

A rotation by $60^\circ$ turns the broken line $APB C$ into a straight segment, which both proves the minimum and constructs the point.
