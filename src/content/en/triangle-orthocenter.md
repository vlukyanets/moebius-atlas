The three altitudes of a triangle, or their extensions, meet at one point called the *orthocenter* $H$. In an obtuse triangle it lies outside the triangle; in a right one it is the vertex of the right angle.

A one-line proof draws parallels to the sides through the opposite vertices: the altitudes become the perpendicular bisectors of the larger triangle.
