---
title: Axial Symmetry
tag: THM
year: 1872
difficulty: 2
requires: [plane-motion, perpendicular-bisector]
---

*Axial symmetry* (reflection) across a line maps every point to the point symmetric to it about the *axis*, so the axis is the perpendicular bisector of each point–image segment. Reflection preserves distances, hence it is a motion.
