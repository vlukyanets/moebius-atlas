---
title: Inscribed & Circumscribed Quadrilaterals
tag: THM
year: -300
difficulty: 3
requires: [central-inscribed-angles, triangle-circumcircle]
---

A quadrilateral can be *inscribed* in a circle exactly when each pair of its opposite angles sums to $180^\circ$; it can be *circumscribed* about a circle exactly when the sums of its opposite sides are equal.
