## Why It Matters

How much warmer is $5^\circ$ than $-8^\circ$? By how much did the
temperature change from $-2^\circ$ to $-11^\circ$? What's $3 - 7$, now
that there's a number for the answer? In grade 5, subtraction stopped
working when the second number was bigger. With negatives, it never
stops: any number can be subtracted from any other - and the rule
that does it turns every subtraction into an addition you already
know.

## The Big Idea

Subtracting $7$ is a move of seven steps to the *left*. So is adding
$-7$. Same move, two names - so $3 - 7$ and $3 + (-7)$ must land in the
same place: $-4$. In general, taking a number away is the same as
adding its opposite. Once you believe that, there's nothing new to
learn: every subtraction becomes an addition, and additions with signs
were the last three topics.

Subtracting a negative is the interesting case. Taking away a debt of
$8$ is as good as receiving $8$: $5 - (-8) = 5 + 8 = 13$. That's how much
warmer $5^\circ$ is than $-8^\circ$ - thirteen degrees, across the zero.

## The Rule

To subtract a number, **add its opposite:**

$$
a - b = a + (-b)
$$

$$
3 - 7 = 3 + (-7) = -4, \qquad 5 - (-8) = 5 + 8 = 13, \qquad -2 - 11 = -2 + (-11) = -13
$$

In particular, subtracting a negative number is adding a positive one:
two minus signs in a row become a plus.

The difference $a - b$ is positive if $a > b$, negative if $a < b$, and
zero if $a = b$. On the number line, $a - b$ tells how far, and in which
direction, you go from $b$ to reach $a$.

## Worked Example

Find $-2 - 11$, $5 - (-8)$, $-4.5 - (-1.5)$ and $\frac{1}{3} - \frac{5}{6}$.

- **$-2 - 11$:** add the opposite of $11$: $-2 + (-11)$. Two negatives:
  $-13$.
- **$5 - (-8)$:** add the opposite of $-8$, which is $8$: $5 + 8 = 13$.
- **$-4.5 - (-1.5)$:** $-4.5 + 1.5 = -3$.
- **$\frac{1}{3} - \frac{5}{6}$:** $\frac{2}{6} + \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)$,
  different signs, size $\frac{3}{6}$, negative: $-\frac{1}{2}$.

The change from $-2^\circ$ to $-11^\circ$: new minus old, $-11 - (-2) =
-11 + 2 = -9$. It fell by $9$ degrees.

## Common Mistake

Turning "minus a negative" into a bigger minus. $5 - (-8)$ is not $-3$
and not $-13$: the two minus signs cancel into a plus, $5 + 8 = 13$.
Think of the debt: cancelling a debt makes you richer, not poorer. And
in the other direction, $-2 - 11$ is not $9$: the opposite of $11$ is
$-11$, and adding it to $-2$ goes further left.

## The Takeaway

Subtracting is adding the opposite: rewrite every $a - b$ as
$a + (-b)$ and use the addition rules. Two minuses in a row make a
plus, so subtracting a negative number increases the result.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $4 - 9$.
2. Find $-6 - 5$.
3. Find $-3 - (-10)$.
4. Find $2.5 - (-2.5)$ and $-\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{2}$.
5. The temperature rose from $-7^\circ$ to $6^\circ$. By how many
   degrees?
6. Someone finds $8 - (-3) = 5$. What's the mistake?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $-5$.
> 2. $-11$.
> 3. $7$.
> 4. $5$ and $-1\frac{1}{4}$.
> 5. $6 - (-7) = 13$ degrees.
> 6. Subtracting $-3$ is adding $3$: $11$.
