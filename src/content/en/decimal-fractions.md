A decimal extends place value past the units digit, so the denominator is always a power of ten:

$$
3{,}25 = 3 + \frac{2}{10} + \frac{5}{100}
$$

A common fraction turns into a finite decimal exactly when its reduced denominator has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$.
