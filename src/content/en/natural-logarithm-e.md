Base $10$ gives $\lg x$, base

$$
e = \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n \approx 2{,}718
$$

gives $\ln x$. The number $e$ appears from continuous compounding, and it is the base for which the derivative comes out simplest.
