$$
(\sin x)' = \cos x, \quad (\cos x)' = -\sin x, \quad (\tan x)' = \frac{1}{\cos^2 x}
$$

The proof rests on the addition formula and on the limit $\frac{\sin x}{x} \to 1$, which holds only in radians.
