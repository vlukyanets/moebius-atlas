The circumcenter $O$, the centroid $G$ and the orthocenter $H$ of a triangle lie on one line, and the centroid divides the segment in the ratio $1:2$:

$$
\vec{OH} = 3\,\vec{OG}
$$

The line degenerates to a single point exactly for an equilateral triangle.
