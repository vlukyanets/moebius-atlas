## Why It Matters

Is the big pack really cheaper per sticker than the small one? If a shop
buys a phone for $9000$ hryvnias and sells it for $11\,500$, how much does
it make? You have $200$ hryvnias - how many $35$-hryvnia notebooks can you
buy? Money questions are the most everyday maths there is, and they all
use the same three or four words.

## The Big Idea

Every shopping problem has three numbers: the **price** of one item, the
**quantity** you buy, and the **cost** of the whole purchase. They're tied
together exactly like distance, speed and time - cost is price times
quantity. Know any two and the third is one operation away.

A shop's side of the story adds two more words. What the shop pays for
something is its **cost price**; what it sells it for is the **selling
price**; the difference is the **profit** - or, if the selling price is
lower, the **loss**.

## The Rule

Price, quantity and cost:

$$
C = p \cdot n, \qquad p = C : n, \qquad n = C : p
$$

Here $p$ is the price of one item, $n$ is how many, $C$ is the total cost.

Profit is what's left after the shop gets its money back:

$$
\text{profit} = \text{selling price} - \text{cost price}
$$

When money is limited, the question "how many can I buy?" is a division
with a remainder: the quotient is how many you can afford, the remainder
is the change.

## Worked Example

A small pack of $12$ stickers costs $48$ hryvnias; a big pack of $30$
costs $105$. Which is the better deal?

- **Price per sticker, small pack:** $p = C : n = 48 : 12 = 4$ hryvnias.
- **Price per sticker, big pack:** $105 : 30$ - that's $3$ with a remainder
  of $15$, so a bit more than $3$ hryvnias, and less than $4$.
- **Compare:** the big pack is cheaper per sticker.

Now the shop. It buys $20$ phones at $9000$ each and sells them all at
$11\,500$. What's the profit? Cost price of the lot: $9000 \cdot 20 =
180\,000$. Money in: $11\,500 \cdot 20 = 230\,000$. Profit:
$230\,000 - 180\,000 = 50\,000$ hryvnias. Or per phone,
$11\,500 - 9000 = 2500$, times $20$ - the same $50\,000$.

And with an equation: a shop sold $x$ pens at $18$ hryvnias each and took
in $234$. How many pens? $18x = 234$, so $x = 234 : 18 = 13$ pens.

## Common Mistake

Calling the money a shop takes in its profit. A shop that sells $20$
phones for $230\,000$ hryvnias didn't make $230\,000$ - it paid
$180\,000$ for them first. Profit is only what's left after that. Always
subtract the cost price before calling anything a gain.

## The Takeaway

Cost is price times quantity, and any one of the three comes from the
other two. Profit is selling price minus cost price - never the whole
amount taken in.

## Check Yourself

1. Five identical notebooks cost $135$ hryvnias. What's the price of one?
2. A cake costs $240$ hryvnias. How much do $4$ cakes cost?
3. You have $200$ hryvnias and notebooks cost $35$. How many can you buy,
   and how much change is left?
4. A shop buys a jacket for $1400$ hryvnias and sells it for $1950$.
   What's the profit on one jacket? On $8$ jackets?
5. Someone bought $x$ tickets at $75$ hryvnias each and paid $450$. Write
   an equation and find $x$.
6. Which is the better buy: $3$ kg of apples for $96$ hryvnias, or $5$ kg
   for $150$?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $135 : 5 = 27$ hryvnias.
> 2. $240 \cdot 4 = 960$ hryvnias.
> 3. $200 : 35 = 5$ (remainder $25$): five notebooks and $25$ hryvnias
>    change.
> 4. $1950 - 1400 = 550$ on one; $550 \cdot 8 = 4400$ on eight.
> 5. $75x = 450$, so $x = 6$ tickets.
> 6. $96 : 3 = 32$ per kg against $150 : 5 = 30$ per kg - the $5$ kg is
>    cheaper.
