## Why It Matters

$84$ stickers, $7$ friends - how many does each get? A $360$-kilometre
trip at $90$ kilometres an hour - how many hours? Sharing something out
equally, or asking how many times one thing fits into another, is
division. It's multiplication run backwards, the same way subtraction is
addition run backwards.

## The Big Idea

Every multiplication fact hides two division facts. $12 \cdot 7 = 84$ tells
you that $84$ split into $7$ equal parts gives $12$ each - and that $84$
split into parts of $12$ gives $7$ parts. Dividing is asking: "what do I
multiply by to get here?" So $84 : 7$ means "seven times what is $84$?"
and the answer is $12$ because $7 \cdot 12 = 84$.

That's also the check. Multiply the answer by what you divided by, and you
must get back what you divided. If you don't, the division was wrong.

## The Rule

In a division the number being divided is the **dividend**, the number
you divide by is the **divisor**, and the result is the **quotient**:

$$
84 : 7 = 12
$$

Here $84$ is the dividend, $7$ the divisor, $12$ the quotient. The colon
$:$ is the division sign; $\div$ and a fraction bar mean the same.

To check: **quotient $\cdot$ divisor = dividend**.

Long division works one place at a time, from the left. Take the leftmost
digits of the dividend, just enough to make a number the divisor fits
into; find how many times it fits; write that digit in the quotient;
subtract what you've used; bring down the next digit; repeat.

Some special cases: $a : 1 = a$, $a : a = 1$ and $0 : a = 0$. But
**dividing by zero is not allowed** - there's no number that, multiplied
by $0$, gives $84$, so $84 : 0$ has no answer at all.

## Worked Example

Find $9342 : 27$.

- **Start from the left.** $9$ is smaller than $27$, so take two digits:
  $93$. How many $27$s fit in $93$? $27 \cdot 3 = 81$, $27 \cdot 4 = 108$ -
  too much. So $3$. Write $3$ in the quotient. $93 - 81 = 12$.
- **Bring down the next digit,** $4$: now you have $124$. How many $27$s?
  $27 \cdot 4 = 108$, $27 \cdot 5 = 135$ - too much. So $4$. Write it.
  $124 - 108 = 16$.
- **Bring down the last digit,** $2$: now $162$. $27 \cdot 6 = 162$ -
  exactly. Write $6$. $162 - 162 = 0$.

So $9342 : 27 = 346$. Check: $346 \cdot 27 = 9342$ - just what the
multiplication topic found.

## Common Mistake

Skipping a zero in the quotient. Divide $4214 : 7$: $42 : 7 = 6$, then
bring down $1$ - and $7$ doesn't fit into $1$ at all. That's a real step
with a real answer, $0$, and the $0$ has to be written in the quotient.
Then bring down the $4$: $14 : 7 = 2$. The quotient is $602$. Skip the
zero and you get $62$, which is ten times too small - the check
$62 \cdot 7 = 434$ shows it at once.

## The Takeaway

Division asks "what times the divisor gives the dividend?" - and
multiplying the quotient by the divisor is how you check it. In long
division, every digit brought down produces a digit of the quotient, even
when that digit is zero.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $1512 : 6$ and check by multiplication.
2. Find $7378 : 34$.
3. Name the dividend, divisor and quotient in $96 : 8 = 12$.
4. Find $3612 : 6$. Be careful with the middle.
5. A $360$-km trip at $90$ km per hour takes how many hours?
6. Why is $15 : 0$ not allowed, while $0 : 15$ is fine?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $252$; check: $252 \cdot 6 = 1512$.
> 2. $217$.
> 3. Dividend $96$, divisor $8$, quotient $12$.
> 4. $602$ - the $1$ brought down gives a $0$ in the quotient.
> 5. $360 : 90 = 4$ hours.
> 6. $0 : 15 = 0$ because $0 \cdot 15 = 0$. But no number times $0$ gives
>    $15$, so $15 : 0$ has no answer.
