title: Affine Transformations
tag: DEF
track: advanced
year: 1748
requires: [homothety]
---

An *affine* map sends lines to lines and preserves ratios along a line, but changes neither ratio of areas nor parallelism:

$$
\vec{r}\,' = A\vec{r} + \vec{b}
$$

Any triangle can be mapped onto any other, so statements invariant under affine maps may be proved for the most convenient triangle, usually the equilateral one.
