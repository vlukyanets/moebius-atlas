title: Axiomatics of Euclidean Geometry
tag: AXM
track: advanced
year: 1899
difficulty: 4

---

Hilbert restated geometry on a complete list of axioms: incidence, order, congruence, continuity and parallels. Points and lines are left undefined, and only the axioms may be used in proofs.

The gaps in Euclid, mostly hidden appeals to the picture, are exactly what this list closes.
