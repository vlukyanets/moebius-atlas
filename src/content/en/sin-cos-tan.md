---
title: Sine, Cosine & Tangent of an Acute Angle
tag: DEF
year: -150
difficulty: 2
requires: [similarity-aa, right-triangle]
---

For an acute angle α in a right triangle, the *sine* is the ratio of the opposite leg to the hypotenuse, the *cosine* is the adjacent leg to the hypotenuse, and the *tangent* is the opposite leg to the adjacent one; these ratios depend only on α, because all right triangles with the same acute angle are similar:

$$
\sin\alpha = \frac{a}{c},\quad \cos\alpha = \frac{b}{c},\quad \tan\alpha = \frac{a}{b}
$$
