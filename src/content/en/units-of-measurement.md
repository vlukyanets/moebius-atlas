Measuring means comparing with a unit. Ten times a longer unit of length means a hundred times a larger unit of area and a thousand times a larger unit of volume: $1\ \text{m} = 100\ \text{cm}$, but $1\ \text{m}^2 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ and $1\ \text{m}^3 = 1\,000\,000\ \text{cm}^3$.
