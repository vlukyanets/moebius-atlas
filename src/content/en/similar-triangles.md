*Similar triangles* have the same shape at a different scale: corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional, with the common ratio k called the *similarity ratio*:

$$
\frac{A_1B_1}{AB} = \frac{B_1C_1}{BC} = \frac{A_1C_1}{AC} = k
$$
