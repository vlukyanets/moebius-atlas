A number is divisible by $2$, $5$ or $10$ by its last digit, by $4$ by its last two digits, and by $3$ or $9$ by the sum of its digits:

$$
3 \mid n \iff 3 \mid (a_0 + a_1 + \dots + a_k)
$$

The digit tests work because $10$ leaves remainder $1$ when divided by $3$ and by $9$.
