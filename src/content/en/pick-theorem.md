---
title: Pick's Theorem
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 1899
difficulty: 3
requires: [area-axioms]
---

The area of a polygon whose vertices are lattice points is computed by counting points alone:

$$
S = i + \frac{b}{2} - 1
$$

where $i$ is the number of interior lattice points and $b$ the number on the boundary.
