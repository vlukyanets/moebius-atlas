---
title: Perpendicular Bisector
tag: THM
year: -300
difficulty: 2
requires: [isosceles-triangle, perpendicular-lines, locus]
---

The line through the midpoint of a segment at a right angle to it. It is the locus of points equidistant from the segment's endpoints - the first example of describing a set by a property:

$$
MA = MB
$$
