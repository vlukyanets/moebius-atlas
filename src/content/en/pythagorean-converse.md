title: Converse of the Pythagorean Theorem
tag: THM

difficulty: 2

---

If the sides of a triangle satisfy the Pythagorean relation, then the angle opposite the longest side is right — the classic tool for recognizing right triangles, as in the 3–4–5 triangle:

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \angle C = 90^\circ
$$
