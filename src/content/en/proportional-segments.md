---
title: Proportional Segments
tag: DEF
year: -300
difficulty: 2
requires: [thales-theorem]
---

Two pairs of segments are *proportional* if their ratios are equal; the generalized Thales theorem says that parallel lines divide any two transversals into proportional segments:

$$
\frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{A_1B_1}{B_1C_1}
$$
