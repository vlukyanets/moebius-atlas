## Why It Matters

"$20\%$ off." "Battery at $63\%$." "$95\%$ of pupils passed." "The bank
pays $8\%$ a year." Percents are everywhere money, statistics or a
phone screen is involved, and they all mean one simple thing: a
hundredth. Learn to turn a percent into a plain number and back, and
every one of those signs becomes a calculation you can do in your head.

## The Big Idea

Comparing parts of different wholes is awkward: is $\frac{3}{8}$ of one
class more than $\frac{7}{20}$ of another? Percents fix that by agreeing
on one denominator for everybody - a hundred. Cut any whole into $100$
equal pieces, and one piece is one percent. $\frac{3}{8}$ is $37.5$ of
those pieces, $\frac{7}{20}$ is $35$ - now it's obvious which is bigger.
The word itself says it: *per cent*, "per hundred."

Because a percent is a hundredth, finding $20\%$ of something is finding
$\frac{20}{100}$ of it, which is $0.2$ of it. That's the whole trick:
turn the percent into a decimal, multiply.

## The Rule

One **percent** of a quantity is one hundredth of it:

$$
1\% = \frac{1}{100} = 0.01
$$

So $p\%$ is $\frac{p}{100}$, and the whole is $100\%$. To write a percent
as a decimal, divide by $100$ - move the point two places left:
$20\% = 0.2$, $7\% = 0.07$, $150\% = 1.5$. To write a decimal as a
percent, multiply by $100$: $0.45 = 45\%$.

**To find $p\%$ of a number**, multiply the number by $\frac{p}{100}$:

$$
p\% \text{ of } a = a \cdot \frac{p}{100}
$$

## Worked Example

A jacket costs $1200$ hryvnias and the shop takes $20\%$ off. How much is
the discount, and what's the new price?

- **Percent to decimal:** $20\% = 0.2$.
- **Multiply:** $1200 \cdot 0.2 = 240$. The discount is $240$ hryvnias.
- **New price:** $1200 - 240 = 960$ hryvnias.

Now a bank. You deposit $5000$ hryvnias at $8\%$ a year. After a year the
bank adds $5000 \cdot 0.08 = 400$ hryvnias, so you have $5400$.

And the other way: what percent is $0.375$? Multiply by $100$:
$37.5\%$. So $\frac{3}{8} = 0.375 = 37.5\%$.

## Common Mistake

Multiplying by the percent number itself. $20\%$ of $1200$ is not
$1200 \cdot 20 = 24\,000$ - a discount bigger than the jacket. The
percent sign hides a "divided by $100$," and it has to come out before
you multiply: $1200 \cdot 0.2$. A percent below $100$ always gives less
than the whole.

## The Takeaway

A percent is a hundredth. To find a percent of a number, write the
percent as a decimal - point two places left - and multiply.

## Check Yourself

1. Write $35\%$, $4\%$ and $120\%$ as decimals.
2. Write $0.6$, $0.09$ and $1.25$ as percents.
3. Find $15\%$ of $400$.
4. A class of $30$ has $40\%$ girls. How many girls?
5. A phone costs $8000$ hryvnias and goes up by $5\%$. What's the new
   price?
6. Someone finds $30\%$ of $50$ as $1500$. What went wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $0.35$, $0.04$, $1.2$.
> 2. $60\%$, $9\%$, $125\%$.
> 3. $400 \cdot 0.15 = 60$.
> 4. $30 \cdot 0.4 = 12$.
> 5. $8000 + 8000 \cdot 0.05 = 8400$ hryvnias.
> 6. They multiplied by $30$ instead of $0.3$; the answer is $15$.
