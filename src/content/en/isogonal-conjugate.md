---
title: Isogonal Conjugates
tag: DEF
track: olympiad
year: 1865
requires: [trig-ceva]
---

Reflect the three cevians through a point $P$ in the bisectors of the corresponding angles. By the trigonometric form of Ceva they again meet at one point $P^{*}$, the *isogonal conjugate* of $P$.

The circumcenter and the orthocenter are conjugate to each other; the incenter is conjugate to itself.
