## Why It Matters

$(-37) + 58 + (-63) + 42$ done left to right is four careful sign
decisions. Group it differently - the negatives together, the
positives together - and it's $(-100) + 100 = 0$ in your head. The
properties of addition you learned for natural numbers survive the
arrival of negatives untouched, and with signs in play they're worth
more than ever.

## The Big Idea

Adding is stacking moves on the number line, and moves can be done in
any order: three left then five right ends in the same place as five
right then three left. That's the commutative property, and it works
for negatives for the same reason it worked for positives. Grouping
doesn't matter either - do the first two moves and then the third, or
the first and then the last two - the endpoint is the same.

Two new facts come free with negatives: adding zero is a move of length
zero, and adding a number's opposite brings you back to the start.

## The Rule

For any rational numbers $a$, $b$, $c$:

- **Commutative:** $a + b = b + a$.
- **Associative:** $(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)$.
- **Zero:** $a + 0 = a$.
- **Opposites:** $a + (-a) = 0$.

So a sum of several numbers can be rearranged and regrouped freely. The
useful strategy: **add all the positives, add all the negatives, then
add the two results** - or pair off numbers that make round totals or
that cancel.

## Worked Example

Find $(-37) + 58 + (-63) + 42$.

- **Group by sign:** positives $58 + 42 = 100$; negatives $(-37) + (-63) =
  -100$.
- **Add the groups:** $100 + (-100) = 0$.

Now $2.8 + (-5.4) + (-2.8) + 1.4$. Spot the opposites: $2.8$ and $-2.8$
cancel. Left with $(-5.4) + 1.4 = -4$.

And $\left(-\frac{3}{7}\right) + \frac{5}{9} + \left(-\frac{4}{7}\right)$.
The sevenths together: $-\frac{7}{7} = -1$. Then $-1 + \frac{5}{9} =
-\frac{4}{9}$.

## Common Mistake

Moving a number without its sign. In $(-37) + 58 + (-63) + 42$, the
$-63$ travels *as* $-63$: rearranging to $58 + 42 + 63 + (-37)$ has
quietly changed a term. The sign belongs to the number, not to its
position. When regrouping, carry each number with its sign attached -
brackets help until it's automatic.

## The Takeaway

Terms can be swapped and regrouped, zero does nothing, and opposites
cancel. Sort a long sum into positives and negatives, or hunt for
pairs that cancel - and move every number with its sign.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(-15) + 27 + (-25) + 13$ in the easiest way.
2. Find $3.6 + (-7.2) + (-3.6)$.
3. Find $\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) + \frac{2}{3} + \left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)$.
4. Find $(-1) + 2 + (-3) + 4 + (-5) + 6$.
5. Which property says $(-5) + 5 = 0$?
6. Someone rearranges $(-8) + 3 + (-2)$ as $3 + 8 + (-2)$. What went
   wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $(27 + 13) + ((-15) + (-25)) = 40 + (-40) = 0$.
> 2. $3.6$ and $-3.6$ cancel: $-7.2$.
> 3. $-\frac{4}{4} + \frac{2}{3} = -1 + \frac{2}{3} = -\frac{1}{3}$.
> 4. Pair them: $1 + 1 + 1 = 3$.
> 5. The property of opposites.
> 6. The $-8$ lost its sign; it should be $3 + (-8) + (-2) = -7$.
