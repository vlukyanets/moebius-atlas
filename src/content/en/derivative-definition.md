The limit of the ratio of the increment of the function to the increment of the argument:

$$
f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0}\frac{f(x + \Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}
$$

The slope of a secant becomes the rate of change at a single point.
