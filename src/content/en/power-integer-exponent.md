Requiring the rule $a^m a^n = a^{m+n}$ to keep working for all integers leaves exactly one choice:

$$
a^0 = 1, \qquad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}, \quad a \ne 0
$$

Nothing is postulated here beyond consistency with the properties already proved.
