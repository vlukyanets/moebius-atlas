title: Tangent-Chord Angle
tag: THM
track: olympiad

---

The angle between a tangent and a chord drawn from the point of tangency equals the inscribed angle subtending that chord from the other arc:

$$
\angle(t, AB) = \frac{1}{2}\,\smile AB
$$

It is the limiting case of the inscribed angle, and the usual first step for proving that four points are concyclic.
