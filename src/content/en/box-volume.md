title: Volume of a Rectangular Box
tag: DEF
track: school

---

Fill the box with unit cubes: a layer holds $ab$ of them and there are $c$ layers, so

$$
V = abc, \qquad V_{\text{cube}} = a^3
$$

The same counting gives the surface area as the sum of the areas of the six faces.
