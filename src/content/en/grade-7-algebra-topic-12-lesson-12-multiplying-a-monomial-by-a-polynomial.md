## Why It Matters

A rectangle $3x$ wide and $2x + 5$ long has area $3x(2x + 5)$ - but you
can't compare that with anything until the bracket is gone. Every
equation with brackets, every area or volume with a variable side,
every step of factoring later on passes through this one move: a
monomial times a polynomial. It's the distributive property with
powers in it.

## The Big Idea

The distributive property said $a(b + c) = ab + ac$: the factor outside
reaches every term inside. When the outside factor is a monomial and
the terms inside are monomials, each little product is a
monomial-times-monomial from three topics ago. $3x(2x + 5) = 3x \cdot 2x +
3x \cdot 5 = 6x^2 + 15x$. A rectangle $3x$ by $2x + 5$ really is a $3x
\times 2x$ piece next to a $3x \times 5$ piece.

## The Rule

To multiply a monomial by a polynomial, multiply the monomial by
**each term** of the polynomial and add the results:

$$
a(b + c - d) = ab + ac - ad
$$

$$
3x(2x + 5) = 6x^2 + 15x, \qquad -2a^2(a^2 - 3a + 4) = -2a^4 + 6a^3 - 8a^2
$$

Each small product follows the rules for monomials - coefficients
multiply, exponents of the same variable add - and the sign of each
product follows the sign rule. The result is a polynomial; collect
like terms if several such products are added.

## Worked Example

Expand $-2a^2(a^2 - 3a + 4)$ and simplify $2x(x - 3) - x(2x + 1)$. Then
solve $3(x - 2) - 2x(1 - x) = 2x^2 + 5$.

- **Expand.** $-2a^2 \cdot a^2 = -2a^4$; $-2a^2 \cdot (-3a) = +6a^3$; $-2a^2
  \cdot 4 = -8a^2$. Result: $-2a^4 + 6a^3 - 8a^2$.
- **Simplify.** $2x^2 - 6x - 2x^2 - x = -7x$.
- **Solve.** Expand: $3x - 6 - 2x + 2x^2 = 2x^2 + 5$. The $2x^2$ terms
  cancel: $x - 6 = 5$, so $x = 11$.

Area: a rectangle $3x$ by $2x + 5$ with $x = 2$: $6x^2 + 15x = 24 + 30 =
54$; directly, $6 \cdot 9 = 54$.

## Common Mistake

Multiplying only the first term, or dropping a sign. $-2a^2(a^2 - 3a +
4)$ is not $-2a^4 - 3a + 4$, and not $-2a^4 - 6a^3 - 8a^2$. The monomial
reaches all three terms, and a negative times a negative is positive.
Draw the arrows, write all three products, then check one value.

## The Takeaway

A monomial times a polynomial is the monomial times each term, added
up - the distributive property, with the monomial rules for each
product. Signs follow the multiplication rule term by term.

## Check Yourself

1. Expand $4x(x^2 - 2x + 3)$.
2. Expand $-ab(a - b)$.
3. Simplify $3a(a + 2) - a(3a - 1)$.
4. Simplify $x^2(x - 1) + x(x^2 + x)$.
5. Solve $5x(x + 2) - 5x^2 = 30$.
6. Someone expands $-x(2x - 3)$ as $-2x^2 - 3x$. What's the right answer?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $4x^3 - 8x^2 + 12x$.
> 2. $-a^2b + ab^2$.
> 3. $7a$.
> 4. $2x^3$.
> 5. $10x = 30$, $x = 3$.
> 6. $-2x^2 + 3x$ - a minus times a minus.
