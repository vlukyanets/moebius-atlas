The area of the orthogonal projection of a polygon equals the area of the polygon times the cosine of the dihedral angle between the planes:

$$
S_{\text{proj}} = S\cos\varphi
$$

Read backwards, the formula recovers the area of a slanted section from the area of its shadow.
