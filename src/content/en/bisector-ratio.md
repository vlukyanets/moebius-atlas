The bisector drawn from a vertex divides the opposite side in the ratio of the two adjacent sides:

$$
\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}
$$

The external bisector cuts the same ratio on the extension of the side, and that pair of points is what produces the Apollonius circle.
