A polyhedron is regular when its faces are equal regular polygons and the same number of them meets at each vertex. There are exactly five: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron - the angles at a vertex must add up to less than $360^\circ$, and that leaves no other option.
