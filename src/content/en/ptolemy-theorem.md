---
title: Ptolemy's Theorem
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 150
requires: [inscribed-quadrilateral, similarity-sas]
---

In a cyclic quadrilateral the product of the diagonals equals the sum of the products of the opposite sides:

$$
AC\cdot BD = AB\cdot CD + BC\cdot AD
$$

For an arbitrary quadrilateral the left side never exceeds the right one, with equality exactly in the cyclic case.
