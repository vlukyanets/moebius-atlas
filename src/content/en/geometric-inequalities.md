Standard machinery for bounding lengths and areas: the triangle inequality, the mean inequalities applied to sides, and area formulas that trade an unknown angle for its sine.

A typical corollary bounds the area of a triangle by its perimeter,

$$
S \le \frac{P^2}{12\sqrt{3}}
$$

with equality for the equilateral triangle.
