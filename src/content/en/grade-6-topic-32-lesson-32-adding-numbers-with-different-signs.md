## Why It Matters

You owe $50$ hryvnias and get paid $80$ - are you in the black, and by
how much? The temperature was $-9^\circ$ and rose $4$ - is it above zero
yet? A lift at floor $-2$ goes up $5$. Whenever a gain meets a loss, a
rise meets a fall, the two pull in opposite directions - and the sum
tells you which one won and by how much.

## The Big Idea

Back to steps on the number line. Start at $-9$ and add $4$: nine steps
left, then four steps right. Four of the left steps are undone; five
remain. You end at $-5$. The two moves fight each other, so the
distances *subtract* - and the direction is whichever move was longer.

Money says the same thing. A debt of $50$ and an income of $80$: the
income covers the debt with $30$ to spare, so the result is $+30$. A debt
of $80$ and an income of $50$: the debt is only partly covered, $30$ is
still owed, $-30$. Bigger size wins; the difference is what's left.

## The Rule

To add two numbers with different signs, **subtract the smaller
absolute value from the larger, and give the result the sign of the
number with the larger absolute value:**

$$
(-9) + 4 = -(9 - 4) = -5, \qquad (-50) + 80 = +(80 - 50) = 30
$$

Two opposite numbers add to zero: $a + (-a) = 0$.

Adding zero changes nothing: $a + 0 = a$.

## Worked Example

Find $(-9) + 4$, $7 + (-2.5)$ and $\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) + \frac{1}{4}$.

- **$(-9) + 4$.** Sizes $9$ and $4$; the bigger is $9$, belonging to the
  negative number. Difference $9 - 4 = 5$. Answer: $-5$.
- **$7 + (-2.5)$.** Sizes $7$ and $2.5$; the bigger belongs to the
  positive $7$. Difference $4.5$. Answer: $4.5$.
- **$\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) + \frac{1}{4}$.** Sizes $\frac{2}{3} =
  \frac{8}{12}$ and $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}$; bigger is $\frac{8}{12}$,
  negative. Difference $\frac{5}{12}$. Answer: $-\frac{5}{12}$.

The lift: $(-2) + 5 = 3$ - the third floor. The temperature:
$(-9) + 4 = -5^\circ$ - still below zero.

## Common Mistake

Adding the sizes and picking a sign by guesswork. $(-9) + 4$ is not
$-13$ (that's $(-9) + (-4)$) and not $13$. Opposite moves partly cancel,
so the sizes subtract, and the sign comes from the bigger one - not
from whichever number was written first. If you wrote the sum down as
money, could you have a $13$-hryvnia debt after owing $9$ and earning
$4$? No.

## The Takeaway

Different signs pull opposite ways: subtract the smaller absolute
value from the larger, and the bigger one's sign wins. Opposites
cancel to exactly zero.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(-12) + 7$.
2. Find $15 + (-6)$.
3. Find $(-3.4) + 5$.
4. Find $\frac{1}{2} + \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)$.
5. Find $(-8) + 8$ and $0 + (-4)$.
6. Someone finds $(-9) + 4 = -13$. What did they compute instead, and
   what's the right answer?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $-5$.
> 2. $9$.
> 3. $1.6$.
> 4. $\frac{3}{6} - \frac{5}{6}$, size $\frac{2}{6}$, negative: $-\frac{1}{3}$.
> 5. $0$ and $-4$.
> 6. They added the sizes as if both were negative; the answer is $-5$.
