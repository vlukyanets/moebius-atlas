Every derivative formula, read backwards, becomes an antiderivative:

$$
\int x^n\,dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C, \qquad \int \frac{dx}{x} = \ln|x| + C
$$

Sums and constant factors behave as they do for derivatives.
