$$
(u \pm v)' = u' \pm v', \quad (uv)' = u'v + uv', \quad \left(\frac{u}{v}\right)' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}
$$

A constant factor comes out in front; these rules reduce every school function to a short table.
