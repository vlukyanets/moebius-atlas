A full turn returns the point to its place, so

$$
\sin(\alpha + 2\pi k) = \sin\alpha, \qquad \tan(\alpha + \pi k) = \tan\alpha
$$

Sine and cosine have period $2\pi$, tangent and cotangent the shorter period $\pi$.
