A map preserving sums and scalar multiples is fully described by the images of the basis vectors, hence by a matrix:

$$
T(\vec{r}) = A\vec{r}
$$

Rotations, reflections, homotheties and shears are all matrices, and composing transformations becomes multiplying them; the determinant tells how areas scale.
