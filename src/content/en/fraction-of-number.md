Divide the number by the denominator and multiply by the numerator: $rac{3}{4}$ of $20$ is $20 : 4 \cdot 3 = 15$. The inverse problem, recovering the number from a known fraction of it, runs the two steps backwards - the same pair of moves as with percent.
