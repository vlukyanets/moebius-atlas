Cevians through the vertices are concurrent exactly when the sines of the angles they cut off satisfy

$$
\frac{\sin\angle BAD}{\sin\angle DAC}\cdot\frac{\sin\angle CBE}{\sin\angle EBA}\cdot\frac{\sin\angle ACF}{\sin\angle FCB} = 1
$$

The angular form works where the side ratios are unknown, for instance for bisectors of angles between cevians.
