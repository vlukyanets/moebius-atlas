Two quantities are directly proportional when their ratio stays constant and inversely proportional when their product does:

$$
\frac{y}{x} = k \qquad\text{or}\qquad xy = k
$$

Speed and time for a fixed distance are inversely proportional; price and quantity are directly proportional.
