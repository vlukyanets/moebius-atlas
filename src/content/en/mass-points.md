---
title: Mass Point Geometry
tag: MTD
track: olympiad
year: 1827
difficulty: 3
requires: [ceva-theorem]
---

Hang weights at the vertices so that every cevian becomes a lever in balance. The point of intersection is then the centre of mass, and each ratio is read off directly:

$$
\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{m_C}{m_B}
$$

The method turns a long chain of ratio computations into a few lines of arithmetic.
