---
title: Desargues' Theorem
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 1639
requires: [menelaus-theorem]
---

Two triangles are in perspective from a point exactly when they are in perspective from a line: if the lines through corresponding vertices meet at one point, the three intersection points of corresponding sides are collinear, and conversely.

The statement mentions no lengths at all, which is why it belongs to projective rather than metric geometry.
