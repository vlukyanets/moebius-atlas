title: Dot Product in Space
tag: THM
track: school
grade: 10
requires: [space-vectors, dot-product-coordinates]
---

The product keeps its two faces:

$$
\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3 = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\varphi
$$

so the angle between lines, and the perpendicularity test $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} = 0$, are computed from coordinates alone.
