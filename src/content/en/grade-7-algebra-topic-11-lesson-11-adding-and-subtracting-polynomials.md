## Why It Matters

The perimeter of a figure made of two shapes is their perimeters
added; the profit is revenue minus costs; the distance still to go is
the total minus what's covered. When each of those is a polynomial,
you need to add and subtract polynomials - and it turns out you
already can. It's the bracket rule and like terms from grade 6, with
polynomials in the brackets.

## The Big Idea

A polynomial in brackets with a plus in front: drop the brackets, keep
every sign. With a minus in front: drop the brackets, flip every sign -
because subtracting a sum means subtracting each term. After that,
collect like terms. That's the entire procedure: two polynomials go
in, one polynomial in standard form comes out.

Going the other way - putting brackets in - is sometimes useful too,
and it follows the same rule backwards: terms enclosed after a minus
change their signs.

## The Rule

**To add polynomials:** write them one after the other with a plus
between, remove the brackets without changing signs, and collect like
terms.

$$
(3x^2 - 2x + 1) + (x^2 + 5x - 4) = 4x^2 + 3x - 3
$$

**To subtract polynomials:** remove the brackets after the minus,
changing the sign of every term inside, then collect like terms.

$$
(3x^2 - 2x + 1) - (x^2 + 5x - 4) = 3x^2 - 2x + 1 - x^2 - 5x + 4 = 2x^2 - 7x + 5
$$

The sum and the difference of polynomials are polynomials. Enclosing
in brackets: $a - b + c = a - (b - c) = a + (-b + c)$.

## Worked Example

Find the difference $(5a^2 - 3ab + b^2) - (2a^2 - 3ab - 4b^2)$ and the
sum $(x^3 - x) + (2x - x^3 + 1)$. Then simplify $2y - (y - 3) + (4 - 3y)$.

- **Difference.** Flip the signs of the second: $5a^2 - 3ab + b^2 - 2a^2 +
  3ab + 4b^2$. Collect: $3a^2 + 0 \cdot ab + 5b^2 = 3a^2 + 5b^2$.
- **Sum.** Drop the brackets: $x^3 - x + 2x - x^3 + 1$. Collect: $x + 1$ -
  the cubes cancelled.
- **Mixed.** $2y - y + 3 + 4 - 3y = -2y + 7$.

Check the difference at $a = 1$, $b = 1$: original $(5 - 3 + 1) - (2 - 3 - 4)
= 3 - (-5) = 8$; result $3 + 5 = 8$.

## Common Mistake

Flipping only the first sign after a minus. $(3x^2 - 2x + 1) - (x^2 +
5x - 4)$ written as $3x^2 - 2x + 1 - x^2 + 5x - 4$ has the second and
third terms of the subtrahend unflipped - and gives $2x^2 + 3x - 3$
instead of $2x^2 - 7x + 5$. The minus applies to the whole bracket;
every term inside changes sign. Substitute a value to catch it.

## The Takeaway

Adding polynomials: drop the brackets, collect. Subtracting: drop the
brackets and flip every sign inside, then collect. The result is
always a polynomial, and a substituted value checks it.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(4x - 7) + (2x + 9)$.
2. Find $(4x - 7) - (2x + 9)$.
3. Find $(a^2 + 2ab - b^2) - (a^2 - 2ab - b^2)$.
4. Find $(m^3 - 2m) + (m^2 + 2m) - (m^3 + m^2)$.
5. A shop's revenue is $5x^2 + 3x$ and its costs are $2x^2 + 3x + 40$.
   Find the profit.
6. Someone finds $(x^2 - x) - (x^2 - 3x + 2) = -x - 3x + 2 = -4x + 2$.
   What went wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $6x + 2$.
> 2. $2x - 16$.
> 3. $4ab$.
> 4. $0$.
> 5. $3x^2 - 40$.
> 6. Only the $x^2$ flipped correctly; the whole bracket flips:
>    $x^2 - x - x^2 + 3x - 2 = 2x - 2$.
