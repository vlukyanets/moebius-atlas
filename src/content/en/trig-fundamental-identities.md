$$
\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha = 1, \qquad 1 + \tan^2\alpha = \frac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}, \qquad \tan\alpha\cot\alpha = 1
$$

They let any one of the four functions be expressed through any other, up to the sign given by the quadrant.
