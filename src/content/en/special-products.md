Three products worth memorising, since they appear in both directions:

$$
(a \pm b)^2 = a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2, \qquad a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)
$$

Read left to right they expand brackets, read right to left they factor an expression.
