A fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ records $a$ parts of a whole split into $b$ equal parts. Multiplying numerator and denominator by the same number does not change the value, which is what makes reduction and a common denominator possible:

$$
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a k}{b k}
$$
