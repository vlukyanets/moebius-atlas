On the *unit semicircle*, sine and cosine of an angle $\alpha$ are the $y$- and $x$-coordinates of the corresponding point, extending the acute-angle ratios to $0^\circ \le \alpha \le 180^\circ$; for obtuse angles the cosine becomes negative.
