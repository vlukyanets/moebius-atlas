## Why It Matters

$25 \cdot 37 \cdot 4$ looks like two column multiplications. It's actually
none: $25 \cdot 4 = 100$, and $100 \cdot 37 = 3700$ - done in your head.
The properties of multiplication are what give you permission to
rearrange a product like that. They're the difference between a minute of
scribbling and three seconds of thinking.

## The Big Idea

Remember the rectangle of dots: $7$ rows of $12$ is the same rectangle as
$12$ rows of $7$, so the order of the factors can't change the product.
Now picture a box of dots - a stack of rectangles. $3$ layers of $4$ rows
of $5$ dots is $60$ dots, and it stays $60$ dots however you slice the box:
count layers first, rows first, columns first. That's why factors can be
grouped any way you like.

The third property is about a rectangle cut in two. A rectangle $7$ dots
tall and $12$ dots wide can be cut into a $7 \times 10$ piece and a
$7 \times 2$ piece. The whole rectangle is $7 \cdot 12$; the two pieces are
$7 \cdot 10$ and $7 \cdot 2$. Same dots, so $7 \cdot 12 = 7 \cdot 10 +
7 \cdot 2$. That's the distributive property - and it's the reason column
multiplication works at all.

## The Rule

For any numbers $a$, $b$ and $c$:

- **Commutative property:** $a \cdot b = b \cdot a$. Swapping the factors
  doesn't change the product.
- **Associative property:** $(a \cdot b) \cdot c = a \cdot (b \cdot c)$.
  Factors can be grouped in any way.
- **Distributive property:** $a \cdot (b + c) = a \cdot b + a \cdot c$, and
  likewise $a \cdot (b - c) = a \cdot b - a \cdot c$. To multiply a sum or a
  difference by a number, multiply each part and then add or subtract.

The distributive property also runs in reverse: $a \cdot b + a \cdot c =
a \cdot (b + c)$. If two products share a factor, you can take it outside
the brackets.

## Worked Example

Find $25 \cdot 37 \cdot 4$ in the easiest way.

- **Look for a friendly pair.** $25 \cdot 4 = 100$ - a round number.
- **Bring them together.** By the commutative property,
  $25 \cdot 37 \cdot 4 = 25 \cdot 4 \cdot 37$; by the associative property,
  that's $(25 \cdot 4) \cdot 37$.
- **Multiply:** $100 \cdot 37 = 3700$.

Now find $48 \cdot 102$ without a column.

- **Split the awkward factor:** $102 = 100 + 2$.
- **Distribute:** $48 \cdot (100 + 2) = 48 \cdot 100 + 48 \cdot 2 =
  4800 + 96 = 4896$.

And in reverse: $73 \cdot 16 + 27 \cdot 16$. Both products share the factor
$16$, so this is $(73 + 27) \cdot 16 = 100 \cdot 16 = 1600$.

## Common Mistake

Distributing to only one part. $6 \cdot (10 + 3)$ is not $60 + 3$: the $6$
has to reach *every* term inside the brackets, so it's $60 + 18 = 78$. The
quick check is the meaning - $6 \cdot 13$ is six thirteens, and $63$ is
clearly not that.

## The Takeaway

Factors can be swapped and regrouped freely, and a number times a sum is
the sum of the separate products. Use all three to turn a hard product into
an easy one - round numbers first.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $8 \cdot 39 \cdot 125$ in the easiest way.
2. Find $56 \cdot 101$ without a column.
3. Find $19 \cdot 99$ using $99 = 100 - 1$.
4. Find $37 \cdot 45 + 63 \cdot 45$.
5. Which property is used in $5 \cdot (20 \cdot 7) = (5 \cdot 20) \cdot 7$?
6. Someone says $4 \cdot (50 + 6) = 200 + 6 = 206$. Where's the mistake?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $(8 \cdot 125) \cdot 39 = 1000 \cdot 39 = 39\,000$.
> 2. $56 \cdot 100 + 56 \cdot 1 = 5600 + 56 = 5656$.
> 3. $19 \cdot 100 - 19 \cdot 1 = 1900 - 19 = 1881$.
> 4. $(37 + 63) \cdot 45 = 100 \cdot 45 = 4500$.
> 5. The associative property - the factors are regrouped.
> 6. The $4$ was only multiplied by the $50$; it has to multiply the $6$
>    too: $200 + 24 = 224$.
