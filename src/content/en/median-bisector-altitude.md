Three special segments from a vertex of a triangle:

- **median** - to the midpoint of the opposite side
- **bisector** - splitting the vertex angle in half
- **altitude** - perpendicular to the line of the opposite side

Drawn to the base of an isosceles triangle, all three coincide.
