## Why It Matters

$15 - (8 - 3)$ and $15 - 8 - 3$ look almost the same and aren't: one is
$10$, the other $4$. Brackets tell you what to do first, but they also
get in the way - of mental arithmetic, of simplifying expressions with
letters, of solving equations. Knowing exactly what happens to the
signs inside when a bracket is removed is what lets you take brackets
out safely, every time.

## The Big Idea

A plus in front of a bracket is harmless. Adding a bundle of numbers
is the same as adding them one at a time, signs and all: $5 + (-3 + 8)$
is $5 - 3 + 8$. Nothing inside changes.

A minus in front of a bracket means "subtract the whole bundle" - and
subtracting is adding the opposite. The opposite of a sum is the sum
of the opposites: the opposite of $(8 - 3)$, which is $5$, is $-5$, and
$-8 + 3$ is also $-5$. So a minus outside flips *every* sign inside.
$15 - (8 - 3) = 15 - 8 + 3 = 10$. That's the whole rule, and it's the
one that catches people.

## The Rule

**Brackets with a plus in front:** drop the brackets and the plus, and
keep every sign inside as it was.

$$
a + (b - c + d) = a + b - c + d
$$

**Brackets with a minus in front:** drop the brackets and the minus,
and change every sign inside to its opposite.

$$
a - (b - c + d) = a - b + c - d
$$

A term with no written sign is positive, so it becomes negative. Going
the other way - putting brackets in - follows the same rule: enclosing
terms after a minus flips their signs.

## Worked Example

Expand and simplify $12 - (7 - 9 + 4)$ and $-(a - b) + (c - d)$.

- **$12 - (7 - 9 + 4)$.** Minus in front, so every sign inside flips:
  $7 \to -7$, $-9 \to +9$, $+4 \to -4$. Result: $12 - 7 + 9 - 4$.
- **Add up:** $12 - 7 = 5$, $5 + 9 = 14$, $14 - 4 = 10$. Check by doing the
  bracket first: $7 - 9 + 4 = 2$, and $12 - 2 = 10$. Same.
- **$-(a - b) + (c - d)$.** First bracket, minus in front: $-a + b$.
  Second bracket, plus in front: $c - d$ unchanged. Result:
  $-a + b + c - d$.

A mental one: $3.7 - (1.7 - 2.5) = 3.7 - 1.7 + 2.5 = 2 + 2.5 = 4.5$.

## Common Mistake

Flipping only the first sign inside. $15 - (8 - 3)$ turned into $15 - 8 -
3 = 4$ - the $-3$ was left alone, but the minus outside applies to the
whole bracket, so $-3$ must become $+3$. Every term inside changes sign,
not just the one at the front. If in doubt, work out the bracket first
and compare - the two answers must agree.

## The Takeaway

A plus before a bracket changes nothing inside; a minus before a
bracket flips every sign inside. Check by evaluating the bracket first
whenever the numbers allow.

## Check Yourself

1. Expand $a + (b - c)$ and $a - (b - c)$.
2. Find $20 - (6 - 11)$.
3. Find $-4 - (-3 + 9 - 2)$.
4. Expand $-(x - y + z)$.
5. Find $2.4 - (5.4 - 1.6) + (0.6 - 3)$.
6. Someone expands $10 - (4 - 6)$ as $10 - 4 - 6 = 0$. What's the right
   answer?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $a + b - c$ and $a - b + c$.
> 2. $20 - 6 + 11 = 25$.
> 3. $-4 + 3 - 9 + 2 = -8$.
> 4. $-x + y - z$.
> 5. $2.4 - 5.4 + 1.6 + 0.6 - 3 = -3.8$.
> 6. $10 - 4 + 6 = 12$ - the $-6$ inside becomes $+6$.
