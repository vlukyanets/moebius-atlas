An equation $ax = b$ with one unknown. Moving a term to the other side with the opposite sign and dividing by the coefficient solve it in one step:

$$
x = \frac{b}{a}, \quad a \ne 0
$$

For $a = 0$ the equation has no roots when $b \ne 0$ and any number is a root when $b = 0$.
