$$
\log_a(xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y, \quad \log_a\frac{x}{y} = \log_a x - \log_a y, \quad \log_a x^p = p\log_a x
$$

Multiplication turns into addition - the property that made logarithms a calculating tool for three centuries.
