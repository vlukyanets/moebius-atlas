A proportion survives swapping the outer terms, swapping the inner ones and turning both ratios upside down:

$$
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \iff \frac{a}{c} = \frac{b}{d} \iff \frac{b}{a} = \frac{d}{c}
$$
