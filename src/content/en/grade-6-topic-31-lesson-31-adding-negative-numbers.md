## Why It Matters

You owe a friend $50$ hryvnias and then borrow $30$ more - how much do
you owe? The temperature was $-4^\circ$ and dropped another $6$ - what is
it now? A diver at $-12$ m goes $8$ m deeper. Debts add up, cold adds
up, depth adds up - and all three are the same sum: two negative
numbers added together.

## The Big Idea

Think of numbers as moves on the number line: a positive number is a
step to the right, a negative number a step to the left. Adding means
doing one move after the other. Start at $-4$ - four steps left of
zero - and add $-6$: six more steps left. You end up ten steps left, at
$-10$. Two moves in the same direction just pile up: the distances add,
and the direction stays.

So adding two negatives is easy: add their sizes, and the answer is
negative. Two debts make a bigger debt.

## The Rule

To add two negative numbers, **add their absolute values and put a
minus sign in front:**

$$
(-4) + (-6) = -(4 + 6) = -10
$$

The sum of two negative numbers is always negative, and it's less than
either of them.

Fractions and decimals follow the same rule: $(-1.5) + (-2.3) = -3.8$;
$\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) + \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right) = -\frac{1}{2}$.

## Worked Example

The temperature was $-4^\circ$ and fell by $6$ degrees. Find the new
temperature. Then find $(-2.7) + (-0.8)$ and
$\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right) + \left(-\frac{5}{8}\right)$.

- **Temperature.** Falling by $6$ is adding $-6$: $(-4) + (-6)$. Both
  negative - add the sizes, $4 + 6 = 10$, keep the minus: $-10^\circ$.
- **$(-2.7) + (-0.8)$:** sizes $2.7 + 0.8 = 3.5$; sign minus: $-3.5$.
- **Fractions:** $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{5}{8} = \frac{6}{8} + \frac{5}{8} =
  \frac{11}{8} = 1\frac{3}{8}$; sign minus: $-1\frac{3}{8}$.

The debt: $(-50) + (-30) = -80$ hryvnias. The diver: $(-12) + (-8) = -20$ m.

## Common Mistake

Letting the minus signs "cancel" and getting a positive answer.
$(-4) + (-6)$ is not $10$ or $-2$: two steps left don't make a step
right, and they don't shrink each other. Two minuses cancel in
*multiplication*, and in a minus-of-a-minus - never in a plain sum of
two negatives. Ask the thermometer: colder plus colder is colder still.

## The Takeaway

Two negatives added give a negative: add the absolute values, keep the
minus. On the number line it's two steps left, one after the other -
further left than either.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(-8) + (-5)$.
2. Find $(-0.6) + (-1.9)$.
3. Find $\left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) + \left(-\frac{1}{10}\right)$.
4. Find $(-3) + (-3) + (-3)$.
5. A submarine at $-45$ m dives another $25$ m. What's its depth?
6. Someone finds $(-7) + (-2) = 9$. What's the mistake?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $-13$.
> 2. $-2.5$.
> 3. $-\frac{1}{2}$.
> 4. $-9$.
> 5. $-70$ m.
> 6. Two negatives added stay negative: $-9$.
