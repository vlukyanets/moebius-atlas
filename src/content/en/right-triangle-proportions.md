The altitude drawn to the hypotenuse splits a right triangle into two triangles similar to the original one; the altitude is the *geometric mean* of the two segments m and n of the hypotenuse, and each leg is the geometric mean of the hypotenuse and its adjacent segment:

$$
h^2 = mn
$$
