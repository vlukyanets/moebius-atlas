Because of periodicity every solution comes as a series:

$$
\sin x = a \Rightarrow x = (-1)^k\arcsin a + \pi k, \qquad \tan x = a \Rightarrow x = \arctan a + \pi k
$$

And $|a| > 1$ leaves the sine and cosine equations with no solutions at all.
