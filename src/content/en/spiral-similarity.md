---
title: Spiral Similarity
tag: THM
track: olympiad

---

A rotation about a point composed with a homothety at the same centre is a *spiral similarity*. Any segment of the plane is carried onto any other by exactly one such map.

Its centre is the second intersection point of the two circles through the matched endpoints, which turns many configuration problems into a single observation.
