For a point $P$ and a circle with centre $O$ and radius $R$, every line through $P$ meets the circle in two points with the same product of signed distances:

$$
PA\cdot PB = OP^2 - R^2
$$

The value is positive outside the circle, negative inside and zero on it, so one number replaces the separate school statements about chords, secants and tangents.
