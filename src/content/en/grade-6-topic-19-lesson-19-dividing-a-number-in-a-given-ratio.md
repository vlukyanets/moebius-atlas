## Why It Matters

Two friends put $300$ and $200$ hryvnias into a lottery ticket and win
$4000$ - how should they split it fairly? Bronze is copper and tin in
the ratio $9 : 1$ - how much of each in $5$ kg? A $180^\circ$ angle is
split by a ray in the ratio $2 : 7$ - what are the two angles? Whenever
a total has to be shared out unequally but fairly, this is the
calculation.

## The Big Idea

Sharing $4000$ in the ratio $3 : 2$ means one person gets $3$ parts for
every $2$ parts the other gets. So cut the total into $3 + 2 = 5$ equal
parts. Each part is $4000 : 5 = 800$. The first person takes $3$ parts,
$2400$; the second takes $2$ parts, $1600$. Check: $2400 + 1600 = 4000$,
and $2400 : 1600 = 3 : 2$.

The whole method is in that picture: count the parts, find the size of
one part, hand out the parts.

## The Rule

To divide a number $N$ in the ratio $a : b$:

1. find the total number of parts, $a + b$;
2. find the size of one part, $N : (a + b)$;
3. multiply the size of a part by $a$ and by $b$.

$$
\text{first} = \frac{N \cdot a}{a + b}, \qquad \text{second} = \frac{N \cdot b}{a + b}
$$

The same works for three or more numbers: divide $N$ in the ratio
$a : b : c$ by cutting it into $a + b + c$ parts.

Check: the two (or more) shares add up to $N$, and their ratio is the
one asked for.

## Worked Example

Bronze is copper and tin in the ratio $9 : 1$. How much copper and how
much tin are in $5$ kg of bronze?

- **Parts:** $9 + 1 = 10$.
- **One part:** $5 : 10 = 0.5$ kg.
- **Shares:** copper $9 \cdot 0.5 = 4.5$ kg, tin $1 \cdot 0.5 = 0.5$ kg.
- **Check:** $4.5 + 0.5 = 5$; $4.5 : 0.5 = 9 : 1$.

The angle: $180^\circ$ in the ratio $2 : 7$. Parts $9$, one part $20^\circ$,
angles $40^\circ$ and $140^\circ$.

Three ways: $1200$ hryvnias in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. Parts $6$, one part
$200$, shares $200$, $400$, $600$.

## Common Mistake

Dividing the total by the ratio numbers themselves. "$4000$ in the ratio
$3 : 2$" is not $4000 : 3$ and $4000 : 2$ - those are $1333.\ldots$ and
$2000$, and they don't even add up to $4000$. The numbers in the ratio
are counts of parts, not divisors. Add them up first; that sum is the
only thing you divide by.

## The Takeaway

To share a total in a ratio, add up the parts, divide the total by that
sum to get one part, and multiply out. The shares must add back to the
total.

## Check Yourself

1. Divide $90$ in the ratio $4 : 5$.
2. Divide $3.6$ in the ratio $1 : 3$.
3. Divide $140$ in the ratio $2 : 3 : 5$.
4. A rectangle's perimeter is $60$ cm and its sides are in the ratio
   $2 : 3$. Find the sides.
5. Two partners invested $300$ and $200$ hryvnias and won $4000$. How
   should they split it in proportion to their stakes?
6. Someone divides $50$ in the ratio $2 : 3$ as $25$ and $16.67$. What
   went wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $40$ and $50$.
> 2. $0.9$ and $2.7$.
> 3. $28$, $42$, $70$.
> 4. Half the perimeter, $30$, in the ratio $2 : 3$: $12$ cm and $18$ cm.
> 5. Ratio $3 : 2$: $2400$ and $1600$ hryvnias.
> 6. They divided by $2$ and by $3$; the parts total $5$, one part is $10$,
>    so the shares are $20$ and $30$.
