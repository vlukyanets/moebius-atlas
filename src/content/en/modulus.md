The modulus of a number is its distance from the origin, so it drops the sign but keeps the size:

$$
|a| = \begin{cases} a, & a \ge 0\\ -a, & a < 0\end{cases}
$$

The distance between two points of the line is $|a - b|$, which is why equations with a modulus split into two cases.
