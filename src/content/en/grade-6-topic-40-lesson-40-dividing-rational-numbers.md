## Why It Matters

The temperature fell $18$ degrees over $6$ hours - how much per hour? A
debt of $840$ hryvnias is split among $4$ people - how much each? A
diver rose from $-30$ m to the surface in $5$ minutes - how fast? Each
answer is a division involving a negative number, and the sign rule is
the same one multiplication uses, for the same reason.

## The Big Idea

Division undoes multiplication: $(-18) : 6$ asks "six times what is
$-18$?" The answer is $-3$, because $6 \cdot (-3) = -18$. And $(-18) :
(-6)$ asks "minus six times what is $-18$?" - that's $3$, because $(-6)
\cdot 3 = -18$. Every division is a multiplication read backwards, so the
signs must follow the same rule: same signs give plus, different signs
give minus.

For fractions, nothing new either: dividing is multiplying by the
reciprocal, then the sign rule.

## The Rule

To divide two numbers, divide their absolute values and pick the sign
exactly as in multiplication:

- **same signs give plus:** $(-18) : (-6) = 3$;
- **different signs give minus:** $(-18) : 6 = -3$ and $18 : (-6) = -3$.

Dividing zero by any non-zero number gives $0$; **division by zero is
still impossible.**

For fractions, $\frac{a}{b} : \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{d}{c}$,
with the sign decided afterwards.

A fraction bar is a division, so a minus can sit in front of the
fraction, on top or on the bottom - all the same number:

$$
-\frac{3}{4} = \frac{-3}{4} = \frac{3}{-4}
$$

## Worked Example

Find $(-18) : 6$, $(-840) : (-4)$, $\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) : \frac{4}{9}$
and $2.5 : (-0.5)$.

- **$(-18) : 6$.** Different signs: minus. $18 : 6 = 3$. Answer $-3$ -
  three degrees an hour.
- **$(-840) : (-4)$.** Same signs: plus. $840 : 4 = 210$. Answer $210$.
  (A debt split four ways: each *share of debt* is $-210$; the
  division of two negatives gives the positive size of each share.)
- **$\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) : \frac{4}{9}$.** Different signs: minus.
  $\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$. Answer $-\frac{3}{2}$.
- **$2.5 : (-0.5)$.** Different signs: minus. $25 : 5 = 5$. Answer $-5$.

The diver: change $0 - (-30) = 30$ m in $5$ minutes, $30 : 5 = 6$ m per
minute upward.

## Common Mistake

Putting the minus sign in the wrong place - or in two places. $\frac{-3}{4}$,
$\frac{3}{-4}$ and $-\frac{3}{4}$ are one number; but $\frac{-3}{-4}$ is
$+\frac{3}{4}$, not $-\frac{3}{4}$. Two minuses in a fraction cancel, like
in any division of two negatives. And as always, $5 : 0$ has no answer,
sign or no sign.

## The Takeaway

Divide the sizes, then apply the multiplication sign rule: same signs
plus, different signs minus. A minus on a fraction can sit anywhere -
front, top or bottom - but two minuses cancel.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(-56) : 7$ and $(-56) : (-7)$.
2. Find $0.9 : (-0.3)$.
3. Find $\left(-\frac{5}{6}\right) : \left(-\frac{10}{3}\right)$.
4. Find $0 : (-12)$. And $(-12) : 0$?
5. The temperature fell $21$ degrees over $7$ hours at a steady rate.
   What was the change per hour, as a signed number?
6. Someone writes $\frac{-8}{-2} = -4$. What's the right value?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $-8$ and $8$.
> 2. $-3$.
> 3. $\frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{10} = \frac{1}{4}$.
> 4. $0$; impossible - no division by zero.
> 5. $(-21) : 7 = -3$ degrees per hour.
> 6. $4$ - two negatives divided give a positive.
