title: Physical Meaning of the Derivative
tag: THM
track: school
grade: 10
subject: algebra
requires: [derivative-definition, average-speed]
---

Average speed over a shrinking interval becomes the instantaneous speed: $v(t) = s'(t)$, and the derivative of the speed is the acceleration. Any rate of change - of heat, of charge, of population - is a derivative.
