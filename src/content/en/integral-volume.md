title: Volume Through the Integral

Add up thin disks: if $S(x)$ is the area of the section perpendicular to the axis, then $V = \int_a^b S(x)\,dx$. Cavalieri's principle becomes a formula, and the volumes of the cone and the ball follow in two lines.
