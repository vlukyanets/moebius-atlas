A *homothety* with centre $O$ and ratio $k$ sends each point $X$ to the point $X'$ with $\vec{OX'} = k\,\vec{OX}$. It keeps angles and multiplies every length by $|k|$, so every figure maps to a similar one.

Circles map to circles, which makes homothety the standard tool for problems about tangency.
