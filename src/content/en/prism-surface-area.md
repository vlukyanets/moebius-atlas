---
title: Surface Area of a Prism
tag: THM
track: school
grade: 11
requires: [right-regular-prism, rectangle-area]
---

Unroll the lateral surface of a right prism into one rectangle: its sides are the perimeter of the base and the height, so

$$
S_{\text{lat}} = Ph, \qquad S = S_{\text{lat}} + 2S_{\text{base}}
$$
