## Why It Matters

You had $500$ hryvnias and spent $137$ - how much is left? A book has $320$
pages and you've read $185$ - how many to go? Taking a part away from a
whole, or finding how much bigger one thing is than another, is
subtraction. It's addition run backwards - and that's exactly how to
check it.

## The Big Idea

Subtracting is undoing an addition. If $137 + 363 = 500$, then taking $137$
away from $500$ has to leave $363$ - there's nothing new to calculate, just
the same fact read the other way round. That's also why you can always
check a subtraction: add the answer back to what you took away, and you
must land on what you started with.

In the jars, subtraction is scooping marbles out. Sometimes a jar doesn't
have enough - you need to take $9$ ones out of a jar holding only $2$. Then
you do the bundling in reverse: take one marble from the tens-jar, untie it
into ten ones, and pour them into the ones-jar. Now it has $12$, and taking
$9$ out is easy. That's "borrowing" - the carry from addition, run
backwards.

## The Rule

In a subtraction the number you take from is the **minuend**, the number
you take away is the **subtrahend**, and the result is the **difference**:

$$
500 - 137 = 363
$$

Here $500$ is the minuend, $137$ the subtrahend, $363$ the difference.

To subtract in a column, line the numbers up by place and go from the
right. If the digit on top is smaller than the one below, borrow $1$ from
the next place on the left: that place gets one smaller, and the current
one gets $10$ bigger.

To check: **difference + subtrahend = minuend**.

Two properties help with mental arithmetic:

- **Subtracting a sum:** $a - (b + c) = a - b - c$. To take away a sum, take
  away its terms one at a time.
- **Subtracting from a sum:** $(a + b) - c = (a - c) + b$. You may take the
  number away from just one of the terms.

Also $a - 0 = a$ and $a - a = 0$. Among natural numbers, subtraction only
works when the minuend is at least as big as the subtrahend - $3 - 5$ has no
natural answer yet.

## Worked Example

Find $5203 - 1847$.

- **Ones column:** $3 - 7$ - can't, $3$ is too small. Borrow from the tens.
  But the tens digit is $0$ - nothing to borrow. So go one further: borrow
  from the hundreds. The hundreds digit $2$ becomes $1$, the tens digit
  becomes $10$, and now borrow from *that*: the tens become $9$ and the ones
  become $13$. Now $13 - 7 = 6$.
- **Tens column:** $9 - 4 = 5$.
- **Hundreds column:** $1 - 8$ - can't. Borrow from the thousands: $5$
  becomes $4$, the hundreds become $11$. $11 - 8 = 3$.
- **Thousands column:** $4 - 1 = 3$.

So $5203 - 1847 = 3356$. Check: $3356 + 1847 = 5203$. Correct.

A mental one: $483 - (83 + 150)$. Subtract the sum one term at a time:
$483 - 83 - 150 = 400 - 150 = 250$.

## Common Mistake

Flipping the digits when the top one is smaller. In the ones column of
$5203 - 1847$, some people see $3$ and $7$ and quietly compute $7 - 3 = 4$
"because that's the one that works." It isn't allowed: the top number is
what you take from, always. A small digit on top means one thing only -
borrow. And after borrowing, don't forget that the digit you borrowed from
is now one smaller.

## The Takeaway

Subtraction is addition backwards: borrowing is carrying in reverse, and
adding the difference back to the subtrahend must give you the minuend.
If it doesn't, the subtraction was wrong.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $8421 - 3576$, and check it by addition.
2. Name the minuend, subtrahend and difference in $900 - 250 = 650$.
3. Find $726 - (126 + 300)$ in your head.
4. Find $(548 + 279) - 48$ in your head.
5. A shop had $1200$ bottles of water and sold $853$. How many are left?
6. Someone subtracted $5203 - 1847$ and got $4644$. What did they do
   wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $4845$; check: $4845 + 3576 = 8421$.
> 2. Minuend $900$, subtrahend $250$, difference $650$.
> 3. $726 - 126 - 300 = 600 - 300 = 300$.
> 4. $(548 - 48) + 279 = 500 + 279 = 779$.
> 5. $1200 - 853 = 347$ bottles.
> 6. They subtracted the smaller digit from the bigger one in every column
>    ($7 - 3$, $4 - 0$, $8 - 2$, $5 - 1$) instead of borrowing. The answer
>    is $3356$.
