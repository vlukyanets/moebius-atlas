---

Besides the incircle a triangle has three *excircles*, each touching one side and the extensions of the other two. Their radii follow from the area and the semiperimeter:

$$
r_a = \frac{S}{p-a}
$$

The tangent length from a vertex to the opposite excircle equals $p$, which shortens many perimeter problems.
