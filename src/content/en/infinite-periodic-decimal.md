When the division never terminates, a group of digits repeats forever and is written in brackets:

$$
\frac{1}{3} = 0{,}(3), \qquad \frac{7}{6} = 1{,}1(6)
$$

Every common fraction gives either a finite or a repeating decimal, and every such decimal comes from a fraction.
