## Why It Matters

The temperature drops $3$ degrees every hour for $4$ hours - what's the
total change? That's $4$ lots of $-3$, and a "lot of" is a
multiplication. A diver descends $2.5$ m a minute; where were they
$6$ minutes *ago*? That's $-6$ lots of $-2.5$. Multiplying signed numbers
comes with one famous rule - minus times minus is plus - and it's worth
understanding rather than memorising.

## The Big Idea

$4 \cdot (-3)$ is four copies of $-3$ added together: $-3 - 3 - 3 - 3 =
-12$. A positive times a negative is negative - four hours of falling
$3$ degrees is a fall of $12$. And since the order of factors doesn't
matter, $(-3) \cdot 4 = -12$ too.

Now the diver. Descending $2.5$ m a minute is a rate of $-2.5$. Six
minutes *from now* is $6 \cdot (-2.5) = -15$: fifteen metres lower. Six
minutes *ago* is $-6$ minutes, and the diver was $15$ m *higher*: $(-6)
\cdot (-2.5) = +15$. Reversing the time reverses the direction, and two
reversals point forward again. Minus times minus is plus.

## The Rule

To multiply two numbers, multiply their absolute values and pick the
sign:

- **same signs give plus:** $(+)\cdot(+) = +$ and $(-)\cdot(-) = +$;
- **different signs give minus:** $(+)\cdot(-) = -$ and $(-)\cdot(+) = -$.

$$
4 \cdot (-3) = -12, \qquad (-6) \cdot (-2.5) = 15, \qquad (-2) \cdot 7 = -14
$$

Multiplying by $0$ gives $0$; multiplying by $1$ changes nothing;
multiplying by $-1$ gives the opposite number: $(-1) \cdot a = -a$.

For several factors, the sign is decided by the number of negative
ones: an **even** number of minuses gives a plus, an **odd** number
gives a minus.

## Worked Example

Find $(-6) \cdot (-2.5)$, $\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right) \cdot \frac{8}{9}$ and
$(-2) \cdot 3 \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1)$.

- **$(-6) \cdot (-2.5)$.** Same signs: plus. $6 \cdot 2.5 = 15$. Answer
  $15$.
- **$\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right) \cdot \frac{8}{9}$.** Different signs:
  minus. $\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{8}{9} = \frac{2}{3}$. Answer $-\frac{2}{3}$.
- **$(-2) \cdot 3 \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1)$.** Three negative factors - odd -
  so the product is negative. Sizes: $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 = 30$.
  Answer $-30$.

The temperature: $4 \cdot (-3) = -12$ - a fall of $12$ degrees.

## Common Mistake

Mixing up the rule for multiplying with the rule for adding. $(-3) +
(-4) = -7$, but $(-3) \cdot (-4) = +12$. Two negatives *added* stay
negative; two negatives *multiplied* become positive. And $(-3) \cdot 4$
is $-12$, not $+12$ - "two minuses" only applies when both factors are
negative. Say the operation out loud before choosing the sign.

## The Takeaway

Multiply the sizes; same signs make a plus, different signs make a
minus. With many factors, count the negatives: even means positive,
odd means negative.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $(-7) \cdot 6$ and $(-7) \cdot (-6)$.
2. Find $0.4 \cdot (-2.5)$.
3. Find $\left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) \cdot \left(-\frac{15}{4}\right)$.
4. Find $(-1) \cdot (-1) \cdot (-1) \cdot (-1) \cdot (-1)$.
5. A tank loses $1.5$ litres an hour. What's the change over $8$ hours,
   as a signed number? How much did it hold $8$ hours ago, compared with
   now?
6. Someone says $(-5) \cdot (-2) = -10$ "because both are negative."
   Correct it.

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $-42$ and $42$.
> 2. $-1$.
> 3. $\frac{3}{2}$.
> 4. $-1$ - five negatives, odd.
> 5. $8 \cdot (-1.5) = -12$ litres; $8$ hours ago is $(-8) \cdot (-1.5) =
>    +12$, twelve litres more than now.
> 6. Both negative means same signs, so plus: $10$.
