---
title: Leg Opposite 30°
tag: THM
year: -300
difficulty: 2
requires: [right-triangle]
---

In a right triangle, the leg opposite a 30° angle is half the hypotenuse - and conversely, if a leg is half the hypotenuse, the angle opposite it is 30°.

$$
\angle A = 30^\circ \;\Rightarrow\; BC = \tfrac{1}{2}AB
$$
