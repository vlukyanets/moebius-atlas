---
title: Angle Between a Line and a Plane
tag: DEF
track: school
grade: 10
requires: [perpendicular-and-oblique, angle-between-lines]
---

The angle between an oblique line and a plane is the angle between the line and its projection; it is the smallest of the angles the line makes with the lines of the plane. A line parallel to the plane gives $0^\circ$, a perpendicular one $90^\circ$.
