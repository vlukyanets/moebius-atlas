Read backwards, the cosine of a double angle lowers the degree:

$$
\sin^2\alpha = \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{2}, \qquad \cos^2\alpha = \frac{1 + \cos 2\alpha}{2}
$$

This is the standard way to remove squares from a trigonometric expression.
