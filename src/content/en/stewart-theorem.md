---
title: Stewart's Theorem
tag: THM
track: olympiad
year: 1746
requires: [law-of-cosines, triangle-cevians]
---

For a cevian of length $d$ splitting the side $a$ into parts $m$ and $n$ adjacent to sides $c$ and $b$:

$$
b^2 m + c^2 n = a\,(d^2 + mn)
$$

Setting $m = n$ gives the length of a median; combining it with the bisector ratio gives the length of a bisector.
