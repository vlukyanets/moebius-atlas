---
title: Isoperimetric Inequality
tag: THM
track: olympiad
difficulty: 4
requires: [geometric-inequalities, circle-area]
---

Among all figures of a given perimeter the disk has the largest area:

$$
4\pi S \le P^2
$$

Equality holds only for the circle, so the answer to every "largest area" question with a fixed boundary length is the same figure.
