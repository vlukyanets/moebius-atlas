Take the common prime factors with the smaller exponent for the greatest common divisor and all factors with the larger exponent for the least common multiple. For any pair

$$
\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b) = ab
$$

The GCD reduces a fraction, the LCM builds the common denominator.
