The points whose distances to two fixed points keep a constant ratio $k \ne 1$ form a circle:

$$
\frac{PA}{PB} = k
$$

Its diameter is cut off by the internal and the external bisector of angle $APB$, which is exactly why the ratio property produces a circle rather than a line.
