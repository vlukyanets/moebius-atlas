Adding negatives to the fractions gives the rationals: all numbers of the form $\dfrac{a}{b}$ with integer $a$ and natural $b$. Now subtraction never leads outside the set, and division works for every divisor except zero.

Signs follow one rule: like signs give a plus, unlike signs give a minus.
