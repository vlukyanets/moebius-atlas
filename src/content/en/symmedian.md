The reflection of a median in the bisector of the same angle is the *symmedian*. The three symmedians meet at the Lemoine point, the isogonal conjugate of the centroid, and the symmedian from $A$ cuts the opposite side in the ratio

$$
\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB^2}{AC^2}
$$

It also passes through the pole of $BC$, the intersection of the tangents to the circumcircle at $B$ and $C$.
