$$
\sin\alpha\cos\beta = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(\sin(\alpha+\beta) + \sin(\alpha-\beta)\bigr)
$$

The inverse move: it removes a product when a sum is easier to handle, and it is how the sum of a trigonometric series is found.
