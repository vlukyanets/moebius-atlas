Identify a point with a complex number. Multiplication by $e^{i\varphi}$ is a rotation, three points are collinear when a ratio is real, and four are concyclic when the cross ratio is real:

$$
z' = z_0 + e^{i\varphi}(z - z_0)
$$

Configurations built on rotations and similar triangles usually reduce to one algebraic identity.
