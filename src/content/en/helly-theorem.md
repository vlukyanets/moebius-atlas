title: Helly's Theorem (TBD)
tag: THM

year: 1923
requires: [convex-hull]
---

If every three of $n$ convex figures in the plane have a common point, then all $n$ of them have a common point.

The bound of three is exactly one more than the dimension, and the theorem converts local intersection data into a global conclusion.
