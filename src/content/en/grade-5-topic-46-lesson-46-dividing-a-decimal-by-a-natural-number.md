## Why It Matters

Four friends split a bill of $58.60$ hryvnias. A $7.5$-metre ribbon is
cut into $6$ equal pieces. A car used $37.8$ litres over $7$ days - how
much a day? Sharing a decimal amount into equal parts is division, and
it works by long division with the point kept exactly where it belongs.
Better still, it finishes divisions that natural numbers had to leave
with a remainder: $3 : 4$ is no longer "$0$ remainder $3$" - it's $0.75$.

## The Big Idea

$58.60$ is $5860$ hundredths. Share $5860$ hundredths among $4$ people:
$1465$ hundredths each, which is $14.65$. So dividing a decimal by a
natural number is dividing the digits as usual - the only thing to get
right is where the point lands, and it lands directly above where it was.
The whole part is shared out first; when you cross the point in the
dividend, you put a point in the quotient; then the fractional part is
shared out the same way.

And when a natural-number division leaves a remainder, you needn't stop.
$3 : 4$: $3$ is $3.00$, and $300$ hundredths shared four ways is $75$
hundredths. Write a point, bring down zeros, and keep dividing.

## The Rule

To divide a decimal by a natural number:

1. **Divide the whole part** as with natural numbers.
2. **When you reach the decimal point in the dividend, put a decimal
   point in the quotient.**
3. **Keep dividing** the fractional part digit by digit.
4. **If there's a remainder at the end, add zeros** to the dividend and
   continue - $7.5$ is $7.50$ is $7.500$.

If the whole part is smaller than the divisor, the quotient starts with
$0$ and a point.

$$
58.6 : 4 = 14.65, \qquad 3 : 4 = 0.75
$$

To check, multiply back: $14.65 \cdot 4 = 58.6$.

Dividing by $10$, $100$, $1000$ moves the point *left* by $1$, $2$, $3$
places: $58.6 : 10 = 5.86$.

## Worked Example

Find $7.5 : 6$.

- **Whole part:** $7 : 6 = 1$, remainder $1$. Write $1$ in the quotient.
- **Cross the point:** put a point after the $1$ in the quotient. Bring
  down the $5$: $15$.
- **$15 : 6 = 2$**, remainder $3$. Write $2$.
- **Remainder left, so add a zero:** bring down $0$, giving $30$.
  $30 : 6 = 5$, no remainder. Write $5$.

So $7.5 : 6 = 1.25$ - each ribbon piece is $1.25$ m. Check: $1.25 \cdot 6
= 7.5$.

Now $2.1 : 5$. Whole part: $2 : 5$ doesn't go - write $0$, then the point.
Bring down $1$: $21 : 5 = 4$, remainder $1$. Add a zero: $10 : 5 = 2$.
Result $0.42$.

## Common Mistake

Forgetting to put the point in the quotient when crossing it in the
dividend. Divide $58.6$ by $4$ straight through and you get $1465$ - a
hundred times too big for a bill four people can pay. The moment the
digits after the point start being brought down, the quotient needs its
point too. A quick size check catches it: $58.6$ shared four ways must
be less than $58.6$.

## The Takeaway

Divide the digits as usual, drop a decimal point into the quotient at the
moment you cross the one in the dividend, and add zeros to keep going
when a remainder is left. Division that used to stop with a remainder
now finishes with a decimal.

## Check Yourself

1. Find $9.6 : 3$.
2. Find $58.6 : 4$ and check by multiplying.
3. Find $1 : 8$.
4. Find $0.63 : 7$.
5. A car used $37.8$ litres in $7$ days. How much per day?
6. Someone divides $58.6 : 4$ and gets $1465$. What went wrong?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $3.2$.
> 2. $14.65$; $14.65 \cdot 4 = 58.6$.
> 3. $0.125$.
> 4. $0.09$.
> 5. $5.4$ litres.
> 6. The point wasn't placed in the quotient; the answer is $14.65$.
