## Why It Matters

You got $34$ out of $40$ - what percent is that? A phone went from
$8000$ to $9200$ hryvnias - by what percent did the price rise? A town's
population fell from $50\,000$ to $46\,000$ - by what percent? Percents
are how any two numbers get compared in the news, in school and in
shops, and both questions - "what percent is this of that" and "by what
percent did it change" - come down to one ratio.

## The Big Idea

"What percent is $34$ of $40$?" is the ratio $34 : 40$ written in
hundredths. $\frac{34}{40} = 0.85$, and $0.85$ is $85$ hundredths - $85\%$.
A ratio is a fraction; a percent is that fraction with denominator
$100$. So: divide, then multiply by $100$.

A change is the same question asked about the *difference*. The phone
rose by $9200 - 8000 = 1200$ hryvnias; what percent is that of the old
price? $1200 : 8000 = 0.15 = 15\%$. The only decision to make is which
number the change is compared with - and it's always the *original*,
the value before the change.

## The Rule

The **percent ratio** of a number $a$ to a number $b$ is their ratio
expressed in percents:

$$
\frac{a}{b} \cdot 100\%
$$

It answers "what percent of $b$ is $a$?"

The **percent change** of a quantity from an old value to a new one is
the change compared with the old value:

$$
\frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \cdot 100\%
$$

A positive result is an increase, a negative one a decrease - or just
say "rose by" or "fell by" and give the size.

## Worked Example

A town's population fell from $50\,000$ to $46\,000$. By what percent?

- **The change:** $50\,000 - 46\,000 = 4000$ people fewer.
- **Compare with the original:** $4000 : 50\,000 = 0.08$.
- **In percents:** $0.08 \cdot 100\% = 8\%$.

The population fell by $8\%$.

The test: $34 : 40 = 0.85 = 85\%$. The phone: $1200 : 8000 = 0.15$, a $15\%$
rise.

One more: a class of $25$ pupils has $10$ boys. Boys are $10 : 25 = 40\%$
of the class; and boys to girls is $10 : 15 = \frac{2}{3} \approx 66.7\%$ -
a different question, a different answer.

## Common Mistake

Comparing the change with the new value instead of the old. The
population change $4000$ against the new $46\,000$ gives $8.7\%$ - wrong.
And the phone's rise of $1200$ against the new $9200$ gives $13\%$ instead
of $15\%$. A change is always measured against where you started. The
clue is the word "from": the number after "from" is the denominator.

## The Takeaway

To find what percent one number is of another, divide and multiply by
$100$. To find a percent change, divide the change by the *original*
value - the one before the change - and multiply by $100$.

## Check Yourself

1. What percent is $18$ of $24$?
2. What percent is $3$ of $12$? And $12$ of $3$?
3. A price rose from $500$ to $560$ hryvnias. By what percent?
4. A price fell from $400$ to $300$ hryvnias. By what percent?
5. A pupil answered $27$ of $36$ questions correctly. What percent is
   that?
6. Someone says a rise from $8000$ to $9200$ is $13\%$, "because
   $1200 : 9200 \approx 0.13$." What's the mistake?

> [!spoiler] Answers
>
> 1. $75\%$.
> 2. $25\%$; $400\%$.
> 3. $60 : 500 = 12\%$.
> 4. $100 : 400 = 25\%$.
> 5. $75\%$.
> 6. The change must be compared with the old price, $8000$: $15\%$.
