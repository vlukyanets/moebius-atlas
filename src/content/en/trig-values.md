---
title: Values for 30°, 45°, 60°
tag: THM
year: -300
difficulty: 2
requires: [sin-cos-tan, leg-opposite-30]
---

Halving an equilateral triangle and cutting a square along its diagonal produce right triangles whose sides give the exact table values, for example:

$$
\sin 30^\circ = \cos 60^\circ = \tfrac{1}{2},\quad \sin 45^\circ = \cos 45^\circ = \tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\quad \tan 45^\circ = 1
$$
