Inversion with centre $O$ and radius $r$ sends a point $P$ to the point $P'$ of the ray $OP$ with

$$
OP\cdot OP' = r^2
$$

Lines and circles map to lines and circles and tangency survives, while circles through the centre straighten into lines, so a tangled configuration can be inverted into an almost trivial one.
