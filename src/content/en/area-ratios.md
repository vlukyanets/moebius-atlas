Two triangles with a common height have areas in the ratio of their bases; two triangles sharing an angle have areas in the ratio of the products of the sides at that angle:

$$
\frac{S_{ABC}}{S_{ADE}} = \frac{AB\cdot AC}{AD\cdot AE}
$$

Most cevian problems collapse once every length is traded for an area.
