Every point of the plane is written as a weighted average of the vertices of a fixed triangle:

$$
P = \alpha A + \beta B + \gamma C, \qquad \alpha + \beta + \gamma = 1
$$

Cevians, ratios and the classical centres all become linear algebra: the centroid is $(1:1:1)$, the incenter is $(a:b:c)$.
