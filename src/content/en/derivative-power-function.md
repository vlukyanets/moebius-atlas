---
title: Derivative of a Power Function
tag: THM
track: school
grade: 10
subject: algebra
requires: [derivative-rules, power-function]
---

$$
(x^{\alpha})' = \alpha x^{\alpha-1}
$$

In particular $(x)' = 1$, $(x^2)' = 2x$ and $(\sqrt{x})' = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$ - one formula covers polynomials and roots alike.
